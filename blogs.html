Solar Installation Cost in Chennai: What Homeowners Should Budget For

A practical guide to the factors that shape the cost of a home solar installation in Chennai.

Read article ＋

The total cost of a home solar installation depends on system capacity, panel and inverter selection, mounting structure, roof condition, electrical work and utility-related requirements. ARTICLE 04Solar guide

How Many Units Can a 5 kW Solar System Generate Per Day?

Daily solar output changes with sunlight, roof orientation and system performance.

Read article ＋

A 5 kW solar system may generate roughly 15–25 units (kWh) on a day with favorable sunlight, but this is an estimate, not a guarantee. Cloud cover, season, dust, shade, panel direction, temperature, inverter performance and system downtime all affect output. Daily production can vary considerably across the year.

Use the estimate as an initial guide only. A more useful projection considers your location, roof layout, shading and equipment specifications. Ask your installer for expected monthly generation and the assumptions behind it. After commissioning, compare inverter monitoring data over time rather than judging performance from a single day.

ARTICLE 05Solar guide

How to Calculate the Solar Capacity Your Home Needs

Use your electricity bills and daytime consumption to estimate a sensible starting capacity.

Read article ＋

Start with several recent electricity bills and calculate average monthly consumption in units (kWh). Divide by the number of days in the billing period to estimate daily use. Then consider how much electricity is consumed during sunlight hours, since a grid-connected rooftop system without batteries does not automatically provide backup during a power cut.

The final capacity also depends on usable roof area, shading, panel layout, budget and local utility rules. Avoid sizing only from the number of rooms or appliances. Share your bills and roof details with a qualified installer for a site-specific recommendation, and ask for an estimate of monthly generation alongside the proposed capacity.

ARTICLE 06Solar guide

How Many Air Conditioners Can a 3 kW Solar System Run?

There is no fixed AC count: power draw, operating time and other loads all matter.

Read article ＋

The number of air conditioners a 3 kW solar system can support depends on each unit’s input power, star rating, inverter technology, temperature setting and running conditions. Other appliances running at the same time also affect the total load. Solar output changes throughout the day, so the system may not deliver its full rated power continuously.

A grid-connected system can work alongside the utility supply, subject to the connection arrangement, while an off-grid or hybrid design must account for starting loads, inverter capacity and battery storage if backup is required. Ask an installer to assess appliance nameplates and usage patterns instead of relying on a simple AC-count rule.

Is There a Government Subsidy for Rooftop Solar in India?

Understand why scheme eligibility and application details should be checked before installing.

Read article ＋

Eligible residential consumers may be able to receive support under the applicable central rooftop solar scheme, subject to the current guidelines and installation requirements. Eligibility, approved-vendor conditions, system requirements and application steps can change, so avoid relying on outdated online figures or informal promises.

Check the official PM Surya Ghar portal and relevant government guidance for the latest terms. Confirm how the application should be completed, which documents are needed and what must happen before installation. Commercial and industrial projects may follow different rules and should not assume residential benefits apply. Apex Solar Systems can explain the installation process and help coordinate relevant information, but subsidy approval is determined by the scheme authorities.

Advantages and Disadvantages of Solar Panels for Homes

Weigh potential savings against upfront cost, roof suitability and maintenance needs.

Read article ＋

Solar panels can reduce the amount of electricity purchased from the grid and help a household use renewable energy. Long-term value depends on the system price, actual generation, electricity tariffs, household consumption and how much solar power is used when it is generated.

There are trade-offs. Solar requires an upfront investment and suitable, relatively unshaded space. A roof assessment and realistic generation estimate help you compare benefits with costs. Request a quote that explains equipment, warranties, expected output and maintenance.

ARTICLE 10Solar guide

How Long Do Solar Panels Last? Lifespan, Warranties and Maintenance

Learn what affects panel life and the routine checks that help a system perform well.

Read article ＋

Solar panels are designed for long-term operation, and many manufacturers offer performance warranties of 25 years or more. A warranty period is not necessarily the same as the panel’s exact service life; output gradually changes and actual performance depends on product quality, installation, weather and maintenance.

Keep panels reasonably clean according to the installer’s guidance, inspect for visible damage after severe weather and monitor generation for unusual drops. Have qualified personnel check the inverter, wiring, mounting structure and electrical protection at suitable intervals. Avoid walking on panels or attempting electrical repairs yourself. Keep warranty documents and installation records so any issue can be assessed properly.
