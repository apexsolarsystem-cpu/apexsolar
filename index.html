Choose Your Solar System

Compare on-grid, off-grid and hybrid systems to find the right fit for your property, power needs and budget.

On-Grid Solar System

Connected to the utility grid, an on-grid system powers your property with solar energy and can export eligible surplus electricity according to applicable utility rules.

Best suited for

Homes and businesses with a reliable grid connection that mainly want to reduce daytime electricity costs.

  • Grid connection and applicable metering approvals
  • Roof orientation, shading and structural checks
  • Typically does not provide backup during a grid outage

Off-Grid Solar System

An off-grid system generates and stores solar electricity in batteries to supply selected loads without depending on a utility grid connection.

Best suited for

Remote properties or sites where grid access is unavailable or unreliable and energy independence is a priority.

  • Battery capacity sized to usage and backup hours
  • Careful assessment of night-time and peak loads
  • Space, ventilation and battery maintenance planning

Hybrid Solar System

A hybrid system combines solar panels, a grid connection and battery storage, with backup behaviour depending on the inverter and system design.

Best suited for

Homes and businesses that want to lower grid consumption while keeping selected circuits powered during outages.

  • Backup loads and battery autonomy planned in advance
  • Compatible hybrid inverter and battery selection
  • Electrical protection and changeover design

Final system selection depends on your electricity usage, roof/site conditions, utility requirements and backup expectations.

PM Surya Ghar Subsidy: How Apex Helps

Eligible households may be able to access support under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana for qualifying residential rooftop solar installations. Eligibility and benefits depend on the current scheme rules and your application.

Subsidy amounts, eligibility, approved-vendor requirements and application steps can change. Confirm the latest rules through the official PM Surya Ghar portal before relying on any specific benefit. Approval is determined by the scheme authorities, not by the installer.
