Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about rooftop solar installation, solar system types and requirements in Chennai.

How much rooftop area is required to install solar panels?+

The required area depends on the system capacity, panel wattage, roof layout, shading and space needed for access and maintenance. An installer should inspect your roof before confirming how many panels can be installed safely and efficiently. This assessment helps determine the suitable rooftop solar system for your property.

What is the best angle and direction for solar panels in India?+

Solar panels in India are generally positioned facing south to maximize annual sunlight exposure, with the tilt selected according to the location and roof conditions. For a property in Chennai, the design should also account for shading, roof orientation and structural constraints. A site assessment can help determine the most suitable panel angle and direction.

What is the difference between on-grid, off-grid and hybrid solar systems?+

An on-grid solar power system connects to the electricity grid and can help reduce electricity purchases. An off-grid solar system operates independently of the grid and generally requires battery storage. A hybrid solar system combines grid connectivity with battery storage. The right option depends on your power-cut frequency, energy needs, budget and connection requirements.

What are the rules for installing solar panels in Chennai?+

Requirements depend on whether the system is grid-connected, the property's electrical infrastructure, applicable Tamil Nadu electricity regulations and installation safety requirements. Grid-connected projects may require utility procedures and approvals for connection and net metering, where applicable. Confirm the current requirements with the relevant electricity utility and your installer before installation.

Does Apex Solar Systems provide solar solutions for factories and industrial facilities?+

Apex can discuss industrial rooftop solar, ground-mounted solar plants and EPC project requirements for suitable facilities. The recommended system depends on electricity consumption, operating hours, available roof or land area, structural conditions and applicable utility requirements. Contact the team for a site-specific assessment.

Every property is different. A site inspection and review of your electricity requirements can help confirm the right solar solution.
