Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers to common questions about solar systems, rooftop installations and solar projects in Tamil Nadu.

01 What is the difference between on-grid, off-grid and hybrid solar systems?

On-grid systems are connected to the electricity grid and may allow surplus power to be exported where the utility permits it. They generally shut down during a grid outage unless specifically designed with approved backup capability.

Off-grid systems operate independently of the grid and typically use batteries to supply power when sunlight is unavailable. Hybrid systems combine a grid connection with battery storage, where supported, to provide more flexibility and backup. The right option depends on your electricity use, outage needs, site conditions and budget.

02 How much electricity can a solar power system generate?

Electricity generation depends on system capacity, sunlight, roof direction and tilt, shading, panel condition and inverter performance. A site assessment can help estimate expected monthly and annual generation for your home, business or industrial facility. Actual output will vary with weather and site conditions, so estimates should not be treated as guaranteed production.

03 How much rooftop area is required to install solar panels?

The area required depends on the planned system capacity, the dimensions and efficiency of the panels, roof layout, access pathways and shading. Usable area matters more than total roof area. A site survey can confirm how many panels can be installed safely and whether the roof structure and layout are suitable.

04 What are the rules for installing solar panels in Chennai?

Requirements depend on whether the system is grid-connected, its capacity and the property. Before installation, confirm the current application, connection, metering and safety requirements with the relevant Tamil Nadu electricity distribution utility. Use compliant equipment and an appropriately qualified installer; additional approvals may apply to certain system sizes or site types. If you are seeking a subsidy, check the current eligibility rules and approved-vendor requirements before proceeding, as schemes and procedures can change.

05 How long do solar panels last, and what maintenance do they need?

Solar panels are designed for long-term use, and many manufacturers provide performance warranties extending to 25 years or more; warranty terms vary by product. Routine care usually includes cleaning dust and debris when needed, checking for shading or visible damage, and having the mounting structure, wiring and inverter inspected as recommended. Follow the manufacturer's safety guidance and avoid climbing onto roofs without suitable precautions.

Every property is different. Contact Apex Solar Systems for a site-specific assessment and guidance on applicable approvals.
